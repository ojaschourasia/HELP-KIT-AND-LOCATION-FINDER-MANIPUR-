Put photos you OWN (or have written permission to use, e.g. from a partner hotel) in this folder.
Then in js/data.js add to that place:  photos:["/photos/aurora-1.jpg","/photos/aurora-2.jpg"]
They will show as a scrolling gallery under the "Photos" button. Keep each image under ~300 KB (resize first) so the site stays fast.
Do not copy photos from Google Maps, Instagram, booking sites etc. without permission.

    🧳 Before you travel to Manipur

    Rules and event dates change. Treat this page as a starting checklist and confirm on the official links before you book anything.

    🪪 Inner Line Permit (ILP)

    Indian citizens who don't live in Manipur need an Inner Line Permit to enter. The state issues a Temporary ILP for tourists and short-term visitors, valid for up to 30 days, and it needs a government photo ID. You can apply online, and it's sensible to apply a few days before you travel. Armed forces members and Manipur residents are exempt. Foreign nationals follow separate rules, so check with the Manipur tourism department.

    🌦️ Weather & road conditions

    Hill roads can slow down or close in heavy rain, and many rural stretches are unlit after dark. Check the forecast and local news before long drives, and keep buffer time for bus and flight connections.

    Imphal weather Manipur Police notices

    🗓️ Best time to visit

    October to March is the usual sweet spot: cooler, drier, and festival season (the Sangai Festival is in late November). Monsoon months bring heavy rain and slower roads.
